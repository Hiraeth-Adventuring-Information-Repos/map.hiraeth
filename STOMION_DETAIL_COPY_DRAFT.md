# Stomion District Copy — Review Draft

This is review copy only. It does not change the live map JSON.

The text below is formatted around the map's player-facing fields:

- `summary`: the short result/search copy;
- `description`: the opening paragraph in Location Details;
- `detailSections`: the expandable story and atmosphere beneath it.

Evidence labels are editorial notes and should not be inserted into the map.

## Academy Gardens

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** A wealthy park district surrounding Hera Vlachouli’s Institute, where manicured gardens, public spellwork, and magical lights create one of Stomion’s most elegant public spaces.

**Description:** Academy Gardens surrounds Hera Vlachouli’s Institute of the Arcane with winding waterways, immaculate lawns, and shaded walking paths. Students practice magic beneath the trees while wealthy residents stroll grounds maintained by unseen servants. Though open to the public, nightly patrols preserve the district’s carefully ordered atmosphere.

**Description:** Aelindor’s Isle rises from the water in salt-darkened cliffs, weathered rock shelves, and narrow ledges softened by manicured gardens. Ancient Castle Aelindor serves as the seat of state administration, reached through a single heavily guarded bridge, while a black Beccin airship hangs above its Elven spires.

## Cathedral

**Description:** The Cathedral crowns the highest point on Stomion’s main island, its beautiful sanctuary open to worshippers of many faiths. Priests of the most widely revered gods tend prominent shrines within the main hall, while smaller statues, alcoves, and offerings honor the countless other deities of the pantheons. ## Coin's Curve

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** Stomion’s heavily guarded financial district, where banks and counting houses overlook an open railway and rumored vaults reach deep beneath the streets.

**Description:** Coin’s Curve sits above the sweeping bend of an open rail cut, with stone banks, counting houses, and financial temples lining the streets along its rim. The clatter of trains rises from below—the sound of money, as bankers call it—while private guards in polished plate enforce dress codes and bar anyone without business. After closing, the district empties into silence above vaults rumored to be carved deep into the bedrock.

## Harrowholm

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped location. High confidence.

**Summary:** A fortified island housing Stomion's mayor and City Watch, haunted by the noble sacrifice from which it takes its name.

**Description:** Harrowholm is the compact seat of Stomion's municipal government. A long stone bridge leads to its round tower, where the City Watch occupies the lower floors and Mayor Thaddeus Vane lives above them.

**Detail Sections**

### Local Rule

The mayor governs with a small circle of appointees, protected from the public by water, stone, and the watch barracks below his residence. Stomion elects its mayor, but Vane's easy cooperation with Beccin officials has left many residents wondering what local autonomy still means.

### The Harrowing

The island remembers an ancient noble family said to have sacrificed itself to seal a gate to the Shadowfell. Their surviving bloodline carried sorcerous power afterward. Guards still tell stories about shadows in the tower and a dungeon holding more than ordinary prisoners.

## Highhammer

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped location. High confidence.

**Summary:** A pristine Dwarf-majority district of forge worship, polished brass, and carefully displayed Ossurian industry.

**Description:** Highhammer is a model district built from imposing stone and polished brass. Its spotless streets present Dwarven industry as disciplined, prosperous, and inevitable, with the great temple of Matrix presiding over the display.

**Detail Sections**

### Forge and Destiny

The High Hammer of Matrix honors the god credited with freeing the dwarves from their ancient oppressor. A colossal ceremonial hammer hangs above the entrance, turning the temple into both a house of worship and a declaration of purpose.

### The Showroom District

Ossurian weapons and industrial marvels gleam behind public showroom glass. The workshops that produce them are closed and guarded. Local artisans and Ossurian expatriates live beside this curated military-industrial display, while the place called the Old Furnace remains part landmark and part warning.

## Merchant's Hill

**Summary:** An old-money residential district of private manors, ornate street lamps, and polite but persistent exclusion.

**Description:** Merchant’s Hill is Stomion’s old-money residential enclave, preserving the fading elegance of families wealthy before the occupation. Each household commissions the ornate oil lamp nearest its property, turning the streets into a quiet competition of glass, metalwork, and family taste. The district is not formally gated, but City Watch patrols politely ensure that uninvited visitors do not linger.

## Newkort

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** A dense residential district of tenements, narrow family homes, bakeries, and food stalls, favored by mid-ranking Beccin and State officials.

**Description:** Newkort is a dense residential district of crowded tenements and narrow single-family dwellings built shoulder to shoulder. Many mid-ranking Beccin and State officials and officers make their homes here, giving the neighborhood a respectable character without the wealth of Stomion’s grander districts. Bakeries and food stalls occupy ground floors and street corners, serving residents from early morning into the evening.

## Northgate

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped locations. High confidence.

**Summary:** A busy, family-oriented gateway district of wagon traffic, traveler lodgings, bakeries, and practical local services.

**Description:** Northgate receives the produce wagons, lumber carts, and overland travelers entering Stomion from the north. Its homes and businesses favor warm beds, hearty food, and useful services over spectacle.

**Detail Sections**

### At the Gate

Beccin soldiers remain visible around the gate itself, but daily life belongs to carters, innkeepers, bakers, and families accustomed to strangers passing through. The district is considered safer and more welcoming than most of the city's transport wards.

### The Lost and Wayward

The House of the Lost and Wayward anchors the neighborhood as both funeral parlor and counseling house. Its doctor-like staff work from open donations, serving the grieving, displaced, and road-worn without turning the district into a place defined only by death.

## Oldfort

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped locations. High confidence.

**Summary:** Stomion's old administrative heart, where a massive Beccin embassy physically overshadows the city's ancient fort.

**Description:** Oldfort is a collision between weathered local stone and the red brick, glass, and bureaucracy of occupation. The Beccin Embassy has been annexed onto the ancient fort so aggressively that the newer structure nearly swallows it.

**Detail Sections**

### Occupation in Stone

Citizens queue for taxes, permits, and petitions while scribes, fixers, and disgraced advocates sell ways around the official process. The district smells of ink and ozone, and its architecture makes the current balance of power impossible to miss.

### After Half Past Four

Oldfort is frantic until the offices close at 4:30. Then shutters fall, clerks vanish, and the district becomes a silent arrangement of locked doors, empty lines, and ticking clocks.

## Smartwalk

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Description:** Smartwalk is Stomion’s working civic district, centered on the State Government Building and local administration; no Beccin offices operate here. ## Steamwall

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and campaign character setup. High confidence.

**Summary:** An industrial utility district of pipes, chemical vents, machine shops, and factories that supply other factories.

**Description:** Steamwall is named not for stone defenses but for its dense curtain of pipes, vents, and pressurized machinery. Sickly lamps burn through colored vapor while the district produces gears, rivets, refined fuel, and the components that keep the rest of Stomion working.

**Detail Sections**

### The Component Heart

Steamwall's workshops rarely make finished goods. They make the fittings, tools, replacement parts, and industrial feedstock other factories require. The noise is relentless by day, and the smell of hot metal and chemicals settles into everything carried out.

### Private Industry

The Beccin state does not directly own Steamwall's works. Private consortiums control the district and protect their contracts ruthlessly. Almost no one lives here; after the shifts change, the streets empty and the machinery cools behind locked doors.

## The Badges

**Evidence:** Existing map copy. High confidence.

**Summary:** A military residential district where Beccin troops, local Grey forces, and their families live among flags and well-stocked commissaries.

**Description:** The Badges houses soldiers and collaborators as families rather than keeping them behind barracks walls. Its decent homes, busy domestic streets, and abundant imperial colors make military life appear stable and ordinary.

**Detail Sections**

### Life on the Payroll

Beccin troops and local Grey forces receive similar housing, encouraging a shared identity built around service. The district's commissaries carry goods that are scarce or expensive elsewhere, making loyalty visibly more comfortable.

### A Public Display

The Badges is surprisingly open rather than heavily fortified. Flags, slogans, children, and well-supplied shops project confidence: the occupation presents itself here not as an army holding Stomion, but as a community already at home in it.

## The Bulwark

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** A fortified sequence of bridges, causeways, gates, and guard stations spanning the vast natural chasm that separates Aelindor’s Isle from Stomion.

**Description:** The Bulwark is not a single wall, but a layered defensive crossing built across the vast natural chasm separating Aelindor’s Isle from the rest of Stomion. Merchant Gate anchors the cityward approach, while narrow bridges, stone causeways, and reinforced guard stations lead toward Governor’s Gate and the final crossing to the island. Traffic can be stopped at several points long before anyone reaches the governor’s seat.

## The Chimes

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped guild locations. High confidence.

**Summary:** A refined artisan quarter of guild halls, luxury shops, surviving ancient chimes, and deliberately quiet streets.

**Description:** The Chimes is Stomion's high-end artisan district, where masterwork jewelry, clothing, and art are selected rather than merely purchased. Old wind chimes still sound over the plaza, encouraging the soft voices and careful manners favored by its clientele.

**Detail Sections**

### A Legacy Name

No defensive gate survives here. The district's old identity comes from the chimes once hung from its eaves and arches. Only a few originals remain, but their music still defines the atmosphere of the central streets.

### Guilded Luxury

Artisan and jeweler guilds dominate local trade. Wealthy visitors, established merchants, and Beccin officers browse uncrowded shops and conclude business over tea, while anyone unable to afford the district's standards is made to feel the difference without being told directly.

## The Hallow Heart

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** Stomion’s central rail junction and water-tower district, named for the famed local inventor Valen Hallow.

**Description:** The Hallow Heart is where the rail lines entering and leaving Stomion converge with the line to the Raildock. Water towers and three great bridges dominate the district, with only a handful of residences standing among the infrastructure. Named for the celebrated inventor Valen Hallow, the junction is vital to both the city’s transportation network and its water supply.

## The Shacks

**Evidence:** Existing map copy and mapped Hags Hovel. High confidence.

**Summary:** A sprawling fringe settlement of scavenged structures, displaced residents, and people surviving beyond official records.

**Description:** The Shacks clings to Stomion's edge in a maze of scrap metal, driftwood, stolen industrial material, and constant repair. It shelters the poor, the displaced, and anyone who needs the city to overlook their existence.

**Detail Sections**

### Off the Ledger

Nothing here was planned, but paths, favors, and local rules give the district an order outsiders rarely see. Homes and businesses change shape whenever useful material becomes available, and formal ownership matters less than who built, repaired, or defended a place.

### Beyond the Patrols

Imperial patrols are rare enough that the absence feels deliberate. That neglect makes the Shacks dangerous, but it also creates one of the few places in Stomion where fugitives and unregistered residents can disappear without immediately entering someone else's institution.

## The Swath

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** A hard-scrabble rail-side district of crowded housing, labor yards, inexpensive taverns, and Stomion’s poorest residents.

**Description:** The Swath is Stomion at its most hard-scrabble: dense, worn down, and constantly shaken by nearby rail traffic. Workers, families, and the city’s poorest residents crowd into buildings repeatedly divided, repaired, and repurposed, while taverns and food sellers offer affordable comfort between shifts. Freight, soot, and vibration from the Raildock shape the district’s daily rhythm.

## The Towers

**Evidence:** Existing map copy, campaign setup, and mapped Oikos Atrium. High confidence.

**Summary:** A warded vertical district of sorcerous houses, sky-bridges, magical lamps, and rumored crypts beneath its foundations.

**Description:** The Towers rises above Stomion's smog in stone spires linked by elegant bridges. Blue-white arc lamps illuminate an enclave where old sorcerous families move between buildings without descending to the ordinary streets.

**Detail Sections**

### Warded Access

Physical checkpoints and magical alarms reveal unauthorized entrants. Private security can strip trespassers of their possessions and remove them, while the Great Houses enjoy a degree of independence rarely tolerated elsewhere under the occupation.

### Above and Below

High-end bakeries, private salons, and family politics fill the towers above. Their foundations are said to contain ancient crypts holding the remains and secrets of Stomion's first sorcerers.

## Wallside

**Status:** Approved and inserted into the map. Summary and description only.

**Summary:** A small lower-middle-class residential district of modest, closely packed homes along Stomion’s outer wall.

**Description:** Wallside is a small residential district built along Stomion’s outer wall. Its closely packed homes belong largely to working households with steady but limited incomes. Neither prosperous nor destitute, the neighborhood is defined by practical housing, careful maintenance, and families making the most of what they have.
